    About this map

    • Source: puddlejump/reference/topology/practice-map.md (draft), uuid faeca3d7-d35d-45fb-8e64-4ac9982cff89. Change that document first, then this map. Each popup names the glossary entry and status document it is drawn from.
    • The line. Python (binary) at the left, Markdown (analog) at the right. A Practice is "code, Markdown, guidelines and guardrails, tended by people"; none is pure code or pure prose, which is why the spiral zig-zags across the line.
    • The spiral opens from the centre in the CTO's order: Contour, Alex, Argus, Meridian, Catalyst, Ariadne. Further out is not better or later; it is wider: from what a kind of thing looks like to how every idea connects.
    • Above the spiral. Puddlejump is meta to the sum of its named parts, the nervous system that holds everything together; its faint threads reach every Practice. Hildegard, the Maker Engine, sits just below and to its right, "Number One", with Catalyst beside them (CTO, 2026-10-25). Hildegard wears green, for viriditas.
    • Marks. Open circles on the spiral are Upkeeps, small tending by hand. Diamonds at the binary edge are Keepers, narrow lookouts in code. Clio, at the analog edge, is Loom's face and not a Practice.
    • Borders. A dashed border is planned, not built (Meridian). A dotted border is on the map but not a Practice (Clio).
    • Colours follow the other maps where a hue is already a concept's: Puddlejump thatch, Catalyst chartreuse, Clio khaki. The rest use the kit's general hues.
    • Not on this map: the other knowledge engines (Daedalus, Heddle and others). They belong on the system map, at its next sweep.
