What happens to an idea you bring into Mixtape?

Maps: System · Loom · Platform · Practice · Knowledge

One idea, from first capture to a way back in. Mixtape reads lightly, offers what it found, and waits for you. You decide what's kept, and everything kept stays traced to where it came from.
