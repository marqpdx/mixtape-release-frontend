About this map

  • Source: puddlejump/reference/knowledge-architecture/system-topology.md v2.0.21 (canon), uuid 4b0fcdaa-ff9c-46e0-9175-76d3c5c4551a. Change that document first, then this map.
  • Names follow the hand-touch glossary: Mixtape is the whole ecology; Catalyst, Crossroads and Tapestry are the Main Gateways, the ways in; Crossroads Place is the reference deployment.
  • Groupings. The map follows three of Mixtape's five groupings: the Main Gateways along the top; Servers beneath them (frontend, core, the services and, along the bottom, supporting infrastructure); and the Layers down the right, Canon and Guidance, Loom and Warp, which run alongside everything. Surfaces and Toolsets, where people do their work, are on the platform concept map.
  • Arrows mean "calls" or "is served through". Dashed lines and borders are planned, not built.
  • Contracts today are REST, plus Celery over RabbitMQ into Switchboard. MCP is still the target external contract; no service exposes it yet.
  • Colours are shared with the platform concept map: the same thing wears the same hue on every map. There it is drawn bold, for newcomers; here it is drawn quieter, for engineers. The Canon and Guidance rail is solid because it is not a component: it runs alongside all of them.
  • Loom is Mixtape's ambient intelligence layer: an always-on background layer of knowledge, kept by people and agents, across Switchboard, Inkwell and Stackroom (it was formerly also called Ambient Intelligence and The Weave): Loom map.
