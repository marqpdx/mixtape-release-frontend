Mixtape is a suite of tools for working in common, expressing oneself well, and building knowledge. You arrive at Main Reception and get your bearings. From there you go wherever the work needs you. What you're working on isn't trapped in any one place — the system remembers it. Intelligent tools help throughout, but you decide.
