Mixtape is built by one person working with a small crew of AI agents. What keeps that coherent is less the code than a handful of written practices: how a new part earns its place, when a step may be automated, how AI work is split and where it runs, how far to extend an agent's remit, and what to do with a glimpse of something not yet ripe.

These are those practices, as they stand. Most are ratified and checked in use; one is still a draft. Each is short. Each paper has three layers: the short summary below, a TL;DR, and the note itself, with a form at the end for corrections, questions and ideas.

The companion packets, *Knowledge* and *From idea to code*, are one level up.
