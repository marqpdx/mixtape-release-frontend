Local-first is the aim, but today's local models can't do every step well enough. Two answers: wait until they can, or **build the decomposed pipeline now**, with every step a real, inspectable unit, and let a cloud model fulfil some steps for now, with consent, while a standing practice measures which steps can move local and moves them.

The paper argues for the second, within bounds that keep it from quietly becoming permanent: a recurring monthly inquiry, a shared table of which step runs where, evidence before any step moves, and every cloud call on the person's own account, recorded. It also asks each cloud step for a short note on how its own ask could be better.

Its companion, *Inquiry Draft as many small calls*, applies it to one pipeline: 24 small per-theme calls instead of one large one.
