Work keeps throwing up glimpses: a direction it could take, a term not yet settled, a shape that may one day deserve study. They aren't tasks, and turning them into tasks would make a backlog. A **Further Refinement** keeps such a glimpse in one dated line, with where it was seen, and asks nothing of anyone.

The note sets it beside its neighbours (a tickler, a change request, a handoff), each of which has an owner and an end. A Further Refinement has neither. AI agents now add to the list too: each cloud step may say how its own ask could be better.
