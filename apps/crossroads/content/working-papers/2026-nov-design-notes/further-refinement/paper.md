**A Further Refinement is an observational unit, not an operational one.** It records something seen: a direction the work could take, a term that isn't settled, a shape that may later deserve a spike. It has no owner, no deadline and no done state. Some are near term and can be acted on; acting on one turns it into something else (a spike, a change request, a handoff), and the Further Refinement records that it did.

How it differs from its neighbours:

| | What it is | Has an owner or date | Ends when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Further Refinement** | A glimpse, kept so it isn't lost | No | It's taken up as work, or it fades |
| Tickler | A reminder tied to a trigger or date | Yes | The trigger fires |
| Change request | A proposal to change a ratified document | Yes | Decided |
| Handoff | Work assigned to an agent | Yes | Resolved |

It is not a backlog (`design-note-avoiding-backlogs.md`): nobody owes a Further Refinement anything, and an old one quietly fading is a fine outcome.

**Where they live:** a single list, one dated line each, with where it was seen. Puddlejump is the only writer; agents propose one in their agent-inbox update. In other documents, mark one inline as *(Further Refinement)* and add its line to the list.
