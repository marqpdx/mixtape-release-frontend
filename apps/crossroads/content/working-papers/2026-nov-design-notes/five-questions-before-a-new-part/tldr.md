Sprawl arrives one sensible addition at a time. So before a new part (a service, a screen, a toolset, a named idea) settles, it answers five questions: is it a **Main Gateway** (where people cross in), a **Surface** (where you do things), a **Toolset** (it travels), a **Layer** (it runs alongside everything), or a **Server** (a running process)?

Write the answers down; look for an existing sibling before making a slot; a part that fails all five is a decision for a person, not something an agent settles. A weekly audit checks that every part on the maps still has its place.

**Names:** *Argus* is Mixtape's audit practice; *Daedalus* is research; *Catalyst*, *Hildegard* and *Folio* are parts that each needed placing after they were named.
