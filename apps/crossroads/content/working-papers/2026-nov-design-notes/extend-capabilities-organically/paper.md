## The principle

Capability grows in small pushes, each one watched. Give an agent, or a person, a little more remit or permission than before; note what happens; adapt the rule or the tool; carry on. Don't design the final permission model up front, and don't hold everything back until it is.

## The loop

1. **Push some.** One step wider than now: a larger remit, one more permission, one more agent, one task handed off.
2. **Note what happens.** Write it where the work is recorded (a status row, a Headnote, the Sheaf). What went well, what surprised, what needed a person.
3. **Adapt.** Change the rule, the brief or the tool. Pull back if it went wrong; it's one step, so pulling back is cheap.
4. **Carry on.** The next push starts from what was learned.

## Why

- **The VPS makes it safe to try.** A server whose state is declared and provable (provisioning), agents that act as named people with a record of each call (Service Trust), and a terminal where many sessions stay in view (Zellij) make a larger remit something to watch, not something to fear.
- **Small pushes are reversible.** A step that fails costs one step.
- **The record is the learning.** Noting what happened is how a push becomes a rule, and how the CTO keeps ownership while handing work off.

## How it relates

- **Manual first** (`decisions/positions/design-note-manual-first.md`) is the same loop for a single step: do it by hand, know it, then automate it. This note is the loop for capability and permission as a whole.
- **Ownership Seams** and **the cadence of knowledge building** say which steps not to push: a seam, where a person's decision attaches, stays with the person until its job is done.
- **The first pushes** (CTO, 2026-11-01): spin off small data-toting steps to agents, so the CTO stops shuttling information between sessions by hand, saving "9 sec 37 times in 20 mins of focused work", while keeping the rote work the CTO learns from.
- **Increasing Coherence** (`reference/patterns/patterns-v1.md`): each push should leave the system more coherent than it found it, or it's undone.
