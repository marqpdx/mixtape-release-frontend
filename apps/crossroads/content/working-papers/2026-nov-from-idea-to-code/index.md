Mixtape is a platform for people and groups who build knowledge together with AI, built by one person working with a small crew of AI agents. Every part of it starts as writing: a position, a design note, a spike, a decision. Those are kept in a single record, argued over, ratified, then built and checked against.

This packet is a sample of that writing, unpolished and dated, alongside a summary of how much of it became code in three weeks. Each paper has three layers: the short summary below, a TL;DR, and the paper itself as it stands. Each paper ends with a form for corrections, questions and ideas.

Two companion packets, *Design notes* and *Knowledge*, are one level up.
