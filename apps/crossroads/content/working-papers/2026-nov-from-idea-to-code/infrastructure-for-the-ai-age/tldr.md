summary: Why generic AI stays generic inside an organization with no canon, provenance or memory, and why that is structural, not a rough edge better models will smooth. Three symptoms (the reconstruction tax, the regression to generic, the silent drift) and the layer that answers them.
---

The argument: a stateless model meeting the stateful work of an organization fails in three predictable ways.

- **The reconstruction tax:** paying, again and again, to rebuild context that should already exist somewhere durable.
- **The regression to generic:** with no curated, attributed body of truth to draw on, output drifts to the statistical average.
- **The silent drift:** documents and reality part ways, and an AI acting on stale documents amplifies the drift with full confidence.

It frames three ages of organizational knowledge (single-copy, proliferation, AI), and argues that in the AI age, discipline about what is true becomes load-bearing. Written in August as raw material, and drifted since in places; it still states the thesis the platform is built on.

**Names:** *Stackroom* is search over a group's material; *Catalyst* holds its canon (mission, principles); *Puddlejump* is our record of decisions; *Switchboard*, *Inkwell*, *Daedalus* and *Livewire* are routing, local models, research and private messaging.
