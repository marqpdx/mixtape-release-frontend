A position paper, written in one sitting and kept as it was:

- **Five realms** hold knowledge by purpose: Initiatives (acting on ideas), Folios (curating research), Catalyst (canon), History (the witnessed record, never altered) and Practice (how things are done). People aren't a realm; they run through all five as filaments, each holding the end of their own thread.
- **One gathering pattern** serves both a collection (a Folio) and an outcome (an Initiative).
- **Attention instead of urgency:** a person rates a batch of items hot, medium or cool in seconds; momentum surfaces, cool things rest in History, and nothing piles up in a queue.

Its naming table is already out of date: AI exchanges are now called *Dialogues*, built the following week (code summary, thread 2).
