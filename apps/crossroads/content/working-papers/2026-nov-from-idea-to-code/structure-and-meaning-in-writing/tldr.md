A research brief, not a plan: what should a system recognize in a manuscript, a Folio or a research collection, and how could it help a person see structure and meaning in their own writing?

It asks five questions (what to recognize, how to process long documents, how to resolve identities, whether relationships can be extracted, how to judge significance), sketches a processing pattern, and imagines what a writer might get: a generated index, concepts traced over time, relationships to explore, assisted curation.

What happened next is in the code summary (thread 3): reviewed test cases, then small local models measured against them, before any model is trusted with a person's notes.
