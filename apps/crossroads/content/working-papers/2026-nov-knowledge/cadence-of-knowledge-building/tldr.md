A system built only to remove friction, aiming at "zero touch", removes the moments where a person actually takes in what is new. The paper separates two kinds:

- **Drag:** waste, to be engineered away.
- **Knowledge-crest friction:** the beat where understanding forms, to be kept, watched, and reduced only once it is well understood.

A good friction point follows a pattern: **notice, name, feel, weave, move on.** First contact with anything new should be a felt win ("First Ones"). Whether a step is drag or crest depends on the person, so the person, not the system, confirms when a step may be handed off.
