Directing many AI agents makes certain human patterns visible. The paper names three realms:

- **Doing:** collaboration, self-expression, knowledge building; the person remains the actor.
- **Thinking:** cognition shows in everyday work; it is often relational, and conversation is a working environment for it.
- **Remembering:** production creates abundance, so memory becomes active stewardship. Everything may matter, not everything must persist; pruning is constructive; canon is one outcome, not the universal destination.

**Coherence** runs through all three: not rigidity, not uniformity, and never free. The longest paper in the set (about 5,800 words), and the most reflective.

**Names:** *Mixtape* is the platform these observations come from; *canon* is knowledge a group has deliberately ratified.
