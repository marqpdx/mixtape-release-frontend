Mixtape is built for people and groups who build knowledge together with AI. These papers are the thinking underneath it: what knowledge is (more than propositions, often beginning as a felt encounter), how it forms, how it is remembered and let go, and how a body of it stays coherent as people and AI produce more than anyone can hold.

They are working papers, most still pre-canon and some long, kept as written. Each has three layers: the short summary below, a TL;DR, and the paper itself, with a form at the end for corrections, questions and ideas.

The companion packets, *Design notes* and *From idea to code*, are one level up.
