---
title: From Phenomena Toward Understanding
version: '0.9'
date: '2026-10-31'
summary: Knowledge doesn't arrive fully formed. It begins in phenomena (a taste, a tear on a bus, a closed factory) and may acquire form over time. Software should support that passage without demanding more structure than a thing has earned.
---

The world produces phenomena abundantly; few arrive as neat knowledge objects. The paper traces the transformations by which an encounter may become understanding: **notice, feel, name, remember, record, share, ask, connect, act.** "Everything matters" doesn't mean everything must be kept.

It argues that knowledge isn't only propositional, that feeling has standing as a way of knowing, that relationship and situation change what is known, and that narrative is a form of intelligence. For Mixtape that means:

- keep beginnings cheap;
- don't demand early classification;
- treat action as evidence;
- keep relational provenance;
- let knowledge stay plural in form;
- treat canon as one outcome among several.

**Names:** *Tapestry* is Mixtape's substrate for situated, narrative knowledge (story meets place).
