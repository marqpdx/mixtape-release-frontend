# Help Hub

Help Hub is Mixtape's central documentation surface. Use it when the contextual drawer is not enough, when you want to browse articles directly, or when you are trying to orient across multiple subsystems at once.

## What you can do here

- Browse help articles directly
- Open a specific article by slug
- Move from page-level context into broader product documentation

## Key concepts

**Contextual help** — The article or set of articles chosen by the `?` button from your current page context.

**Help Hub** — The full browseable documentation surface, independent of where you started.

## Current limitations

- Coverage is still being expanded across the full authenticated app.
- Some pages currently inherit subsystem-level help before they get dedicated page-specific articles.

## Related features

- **Dashboard** — Many people return to Help Hub from their normal working surfaces when they need broader orientation.
- **Admin** — Admin and internal pages can also route here when they need system-level guidance.
