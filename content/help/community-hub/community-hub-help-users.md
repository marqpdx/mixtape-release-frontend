# Community Hub

Community Hub is a shared orientation surface for activity and coordination across communities. Use it when you need a broader view than a single group or a single personal workspace.

## What you can do here

- Review shared activity and signals
- Re-enter cross-community work from a common hub
- Move from overview into more specific group or member surfaces

## Current limitations

- This surface is still evolving and may not yet expose every community signal in one place.
- Some actions still route you out to more specialized pages to complete the task.

## Related features

- **Groups** — Group-specific work continues in the group surface.
- **Dashboard** — Your personal re-entry flow may start elsewhere and then widen into Community Hub.
