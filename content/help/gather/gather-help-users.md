# Gather

Gather rooms are live session spaces inside Mixtape. When a group, initiative, or event opens a live room, the gather page connects you to that session so you can participate in real time.

---

## What you can do here

- Join a live room from a session link
- Talk with other participants in real time
- Use the room controls to manage your microphone, camera, and audio
- Leave the room when the session is finished

---

## Key concepts

**Gather room** — A live conversation space connected to a specific session.

**Session link** — The route that opens the room. If you have access, Mixtape uses it to request a room token and connect you.

**Room controls** — The buttons inside the live room for microphone, camera, audio, and leaving the session.

---

## How to join a gather room

1. Open the gather link from the session, event, or invitation.
2. Wait while Mixtape connects to the live room.
3. Use the room controls to turn your microphone or camera on or off.
4. Leave the room when you are done.

---

## Current limitations

- **Access depends on the session:** If the session is unavailable or you do not have permission to join, Mixtape shows an unable-to-join message.
- **Room context is minimal:** The current room page focuses on joining the live conversation and may not show the full event, group, or initiative context yet.

---

## Related features

- **Almanac** — Events and gatherings may point members into live rooms.
- **Initiatives** — Working sessions may use live rooms for real-time discussion.
- **Chat** — Messaging remains the place for ongoing text and voice-message conversations outside the live room.
