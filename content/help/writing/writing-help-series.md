# Writing — Series

Series let you group writing pieces into a named sequence — a phase, a run, a themed collection. Once pieces are in a series, you can order them, track their status, and navigate between them as a set.

---

## Where series appear

Series show up in two places in the group dashboard:

**Writing → By Series tab**
The standard Writing area has a grouping mode selector at the top right. Switch to **By Series** to see all your pieces organised into their series. A narrow navigation panel appears on the left.

**Series Writing (admin)**
A dedicated work area designed for series-focused editing. It's always in series mode — no tab switching. Look for **Series Writing ✦** in the Content section of the left nav.

---

## The series navigator

When you're in either series view, a panel on the left lists every series for the group. Click any entry to focus on that series. The piece count next to each name updates as pieces move in and out.

- **All pieces** — shows everything, all series and unassigned, at once
- **[Series name]** — filters to just that series
- **Unassigned** — shows pieces not yet placed in any series

Your selection persists while you stay in the view. Click **All pieces** to return to the full overview.

---

## Series headers and health badges

Each series in the main panel has a header bar. On the right side of the header you'll see small badges:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `3 pub` | 3 pieces are published |
| `1 sched` | 1 piece is scheduled |
| `2 draft` | 2 pieces are still in draft |

The total piece count is also shown. These update immediately when you move or publish a piece.

---

## Ordering pieces within a series

Pieces in a series are ordered — they have a position number (1, 2, 3…) shown to the left of each title.

To reorder: grab the **drag handle** (the grip icon on the far left of a row) and drag the piece to its new position. The order is saved automatically. A small "Saving…" indicator appears briefly while the change is being written.

---

## Moving a piece to a different series

Each piece row has a small **series selector** — a compact dropdown that shows the piece's current series assignment. It appears to the right of the title.

To reassign a piece:

1. Click the selector on any piece row.
2. Choose a different series from the list, or choose **Unassigned** to remove it from all series.
3. The piece moves immediately — no save button needed.

The piece count in the navigator updates to reflect the change.

---

## Creating a new series

At the bottom of the series view, there is a **New series title…** input and an **Add series** button.

Type a name and press **Enter** or click **Add series**. The series is created immediately and appears in the navigator. It starts empty — you can then assign pieces to it using the series selector on each piece row.

There is no limit on the number of series in a group.

---

## What series affect

- **Order** — series order is separate from publish date. Use it to define a reading sequence that is independent of when things were written.
- **The public catalog** — your group's public writing page groups pieces by series, in series order. Unassigned pieces appear in a separate section at the bottom.
- **Nothing else** — series have no effect on who can see a piece, whether it's published, or any other settings.

---

## What series do not do (yet)

- **Bulk publish.** Publishing each piece in a series is still done individually.
- **Series-level descriptions.** A subtitle or intro for the series as a whole is not editable from this view.
- **Drag between series.** Moving a piece to a different series uses the dropdown selector, not drag-and-drop. Drag is for ordering *within* a series only.

---

## Quick reference

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| View pieces by series | Writing → By Series tab |
| Filter to one series | Click its name in the left navigator |
| Reorder within a series | Drag the grip handle |
| Move piece to another series | Use the series dropdown on the piece row |
| Remove from all series | Set the dropdown to "Unassigned" |
| Create a new series | Type a title in the field at the bottom → Add series |
| See publish/draft counts | Health badges on each series header |

---

*Series are a way to bring shape to a body of work. They're optional — pieces don't need to be in a series to be written, published, or found.*
