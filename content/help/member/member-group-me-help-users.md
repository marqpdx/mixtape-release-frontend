# Your Group Member Profile

This is the profile edit surface you open from inside a group. It lets you update your member profile without navigating away from the group you're in.

---

## How to get here

Click the **Edit profile** button in the group header. It appears next to the group name on every page inside a group (you must be a member to see it). After saving, you return to where you were.

---

## What you can edit here

| Field | What it's for |
|-------|--------------|
| Display Name | How your name appears across Mixtape |
| Quick Intro | A short bio shown on your profile and member cards (up to 300 characters, required) |
| Right Now | A line about what you're focused on at the moment |
| Location | City, region, or "Remote" |
| Quick Link | A personal or work URL shown on your member card |
| Voice Intro | A short audio clip that plays on your profile |
| Profile Photo | The image used as your avatar |
| Banner Image | The wide image shown across the top of your profile |

### A note on Skills, Bio, and Work Areas

Some community members expect to fill in their skills, bio, and areas of focus here. These fields are visible on member profiles and member cards — but they cannot be edited in this release. They will return in an upcoming update.

---

## Voice intro

Your voice intro is an audio clip that plays on your public profile page. Visitors can listen and read an auto-generated transcript.

To add or update your voice intro:
1. Find the **Intro Voice Note** field in the edit form
2. Click **Upload audio** and choose an audio file (most formats accepted, up to 25 MB)
3. The transcript is generated automatically — it usually appears within a minute

Use **Replace** to update an existing clip, or **Remove** to delete it.

---

## Typical flow

1. Click **Edit profile** in the group header
2. Update the fields you want to change
3. Click **Save Changes**
4. Your browser returns you to the group

If you change your mind, click **Reset** to restore the last saved values, or **Cancel** to exit without saving.

---

## This page vs. your public profile

This is an edit surface, not a public profile. Other members see your profile at `/member/[username]`, which shows what you've published here. Changes you save here update that public view.
