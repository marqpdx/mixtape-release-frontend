# Notifications

Notifications is the place to review updates, alerts, and follow-up cues from across Mixtape. It is where you catch up on what changed and decide which surface to enter next.

## What you can do here

- Review recent updates
- Open the item or workflow that generated the notification
- Clear or move through time-sensitive follow-up cues

## Current limitations

- Notifications may summarize an event but still require you to open another page to act on it.
- Notification language can differ slightly depending on which subsystem produced the update.

## Related features

- **Dashboard** — Dashboard gives broader re-entry context; notifications focus on discrete updates.
- **Groups** — Many alerts lead back into a specific community workflow.
