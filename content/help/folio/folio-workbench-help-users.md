# Folio Workbench

The Workbench is where you look over the notes you've captured for a Folio and give them a little order. Notes are captured in the **Folio** tab of the mobile app; the Workbench is for reviewing them at a larger screen. It is not a manuscript editor — arranging your work into Chapters and Scenes happens in **Storyboard**.

## What you can do here

- See all your Folios on **Folio**, create a new one, and open one to see its notes
- Browse a Folio's notes by **Recent**, by **Shape**, or by an **Entity** you've linked
- Search your notes by your exact **Words**, or by **Meaning** to find notes about the same idea worded differently
- Correct a note's Shape with one click, or drag a note onto a Shape in the left column
- Link the people, places and things a note mentions to Entities you keep track of
- See **Related** notes — the notes in this Folio closest in meaning to the one you're reading
- Move a note to a different Folio
- Look at a note's **Provenance**: what you captured, what transcription and the model added, and what you changed

## Key concepts

**Shape** — A broad role a note plays: Character, Scene, Plot, Setting, World, or Meta. Mixtape suggests one after you capture a note (shown with ▾); when you choose one yourself it's marked ✓ and the suggestion is kept only for the record. Selecting your choice again returns the note to the suggestion.

**Unplaced** — A note whose role is still open. That's a normal, healthy place for a thought to be — not a list of things to fix.

**Mentions and Entities** — Mixtape points out names and things a note mentions, but never links them on its own. When you link a mention to an Entity — an existing one, or a new one you name — that Entity is the same one Storyboard uses for participants, and Mixtape will recognize the same wording in future notes.

**Related** — Notes that are close in meaning to the one you're reading. It's a way to find things, not a claim that they're connected.

## How to organize a Folio's notes

1. Open **Folio** in the main navigation and choose a Folio.
2. Pick **Recent**, a Shape, or an Entity in the left column; or type in the search box and choose **Words** or **Meaning**.
3. Select a note to see it in full on the right.
4. Change its Shape with the Shape buttons, or drag the note from the list onto a Shape in the left column.
5. Under **Mentions**, choose an existing Entity or **New entity…**, adjust the name if you like, and select **Link**. Select **Unlink** to undo it.
6. To send a note to another Folio, use **Move to Folio**.

## What this build does not do yet

- You can't capture or edit notes here — capture happens in the mobile app, and a note's words are kept exactly as captured.
- Meaning search and Related notes depend on a background search service. If it's unavailable, Words search still works.
- Notes aren't arranged into Chapters or Scenes here; link notes to a Storyboard's items from the Storyboard itself.
