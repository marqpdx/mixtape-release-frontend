# Dashboard

Dashboard is your authenticated home base. It is the place to re-enter Mixtape, see what has changed, and move into the work that currently needs your attention.

## What you can do here

- See recent activity and items that need follow-up
- Re-enter work you were already doing
- Navigate into groups, writing, notifications, and other focused surfaces

## Key concepts

**Re-entry** — Getting back into active work without having to rediscover context manually.

**Orientation** — The combination of recent activity, signals, and navigation cues that help you decide what to do next.

## Current limitations

- Dashboard is still a hub rather than a complete destination for every task.
- Some panels may reflect only part of a larger workflow and send you elsewhere to complete it.

## Related features

- **Console** — Personal capture and follow-up can feed your dashboard re-entry flow.
- **Notifications** — Time-sensitive updates often continue in the notifications surface.
- **Groups** — Group-specific work begins when you leave the dashboard and enter a group.
