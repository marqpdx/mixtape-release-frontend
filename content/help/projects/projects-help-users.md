# Projects

Projects is Mixtape's task management tool for groups. Groups can create projects with Kanban boards, organize tasks into columns, and track work across the group. It's designed for members of a group to coordinate action — integrated into the group context, not a general-purpose project management tool.

---

## Key concepts

**Project** — A named workspace with a Kanban board, belonging to a group. Projects can be active, archived, or hidden.

**Column** — A stage on the Kanban board (e.g. "To Do", "In Progress", "Done"). Projects have one or more columns.

**Task** — A unit of work placed in a column. Tasks have a position within their column.

**Kanban Board** — The visual layout of all columns and tasks within a project.

**Hidden** — A project that is not visible to regular members. Used to manage visibility before a project is ready to share.

---

## What you can do

### View your group's projects

- You must be a member of the group.
- Navigate to your group's Projects section.
- All active, visible projects appear here with their Kanban boards.

### View a Kanban board

- Open a project to see its columns and tasks.
- Tasks are shown in order within each column.

---

## Current limitations

- **No personal projects:** Projects are always group-owned. There is no personal project workspace.

---

## Related features

- **Groups** — Projects are scoped to groups. Group membership is required for access.
- **Activity** — Task updates and project creation may generate activity feed entries.
