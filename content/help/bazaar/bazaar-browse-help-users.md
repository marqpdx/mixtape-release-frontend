# Bazaar Browsing

These pages are the storefront side of Bazaar. You use them to browse what is available, inspect a specific offering, and decide whether to place an order.

## What you are looking at

- **Bazaar listing** shows the general marketplace
- **Offering detail** shows one offer in full, including price, availability, fulfillment type, and seller
- **Group stall** shows offerings published by one group
- **Member stall** shows offerings published by one member

## What you can do here

- Browse current offerings
- Open a specific offering to review details
- See who is providing the offering
- Check whether the offer is free, paid, available, or unavailable
- Start a purchase or claim flow from the offering page

## Key concepts

**Offering** — The individual thing being sold or claimed. It may be a service, event, program, or product.

**Stall** — A seller-specific storefront page. Some stalls belong to groups, others to individual members.

**Fulfillment type** — How the offer is delivered after you order it. In the current UI this may be instant, manual, or external.

## Typical flow

1. Start in Bazaar or open a group/member stall.
2. Scan the available offerings.
3. Open the offering that looks relevant.
4. Review the summary, description, price, and fulfillment notes.
5. Use the purchase button if the offering is currently available.

## How to read the page

On an offering page, the right-side purchase card is the action area:

- It shows the current price or `FREE`
- It shows whether the offer can be purchased now
- It is where the checkout flow begins

The main body explains what the offer is, who it comes from, and what kind of fulfillment to expect.

## Current limitations

- Some storefront pages are functional before they are polished. Expect operational language in places.
- Availability and fulfillment language is still being normalized across different offering types.
