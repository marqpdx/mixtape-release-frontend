# Bazaar Vendor Operations

These pages are the seller-side view of Bazaar. If you publish offerings, this is where you monitor demand and work orders through fulfillment.

## What you can do here

- Review high-level vendor stats
- Check which orders need attention
- Open a specific vendor order
- Add a vendor note
- Advance an order through fulfillment steps

## What the dashboard is for

The vendor dashboard is a quick operational summary. It shows:

- total orders
- active offerings
- total offerings
- orders that need attention

The recent-orders area is the fastest way to spot work that needs handling now.

## What the vendor order page is for

The vendor order page is the working surface for one order. It shows:

- order status
- order amount
- the buyer's note, if they left one
- a timeline of where the order is in fulfillment
- actions you can take next

## Typical flow

1. Open the vendor dashboard.
2. Check the orders or attention counts.
3. Open an individual order.
4. Read the buyer note and the order details.
5. Add a vendor note if needed and move the order to its next status.

## Current limitations

- Vendor actions are practical and operational, but not heavily guided yet.
- Product and offering management still lives partly outside this dashboard, so you may need to return to a group or member surface for setup work.
