# Dispatch

Dispatch is the outbound communication surface for creating and preparing posts. Use it when you are assembling a message or publication artifact that is meant to be sent out rather than kept as an internal draft.

## What you can do here

- Create a new post
- Prepare content for outward communication
- Move from internal drafting into a more distribution-oriented workflow

## Current limitations

- Dispatch is still developing its longer-lived conventions and may feel closer to a specialized workflow than a polished publishing studio.
- Related approval or distribution rules may still live in adjacent team process rather than on-page explanation.

## Related features

- **Writing** — Many dispatchable artifacts begin life in the writing system.
- **Help** — Use Help Hub when you need broader orientation across publishing-related surfaces.
