# Dispatch

Dispatch is the outbound communication surface for creating and preparing posts. Right now, only **creating** a post is reachable — the page for creating a new post, at **Dispatch → New post**.

## What you can do here

- Create a new post

## Current limitations

- **Only the create page is live.** Viewing a published post, an author's Dispatch page, or a list of posts are not reachable yet — those surfaces exist but aren't wired up for members to use.
- Dispatch is still developing its longer-lived conventions and may feel closer to a specialized workflow than a polished publishing studio.
- Related approval or distribution rules may still live in adjacent team process rather than on-page explanation.

## Related features

- **Writing** — Many dispatchable artifacts begin life in the writing system.
