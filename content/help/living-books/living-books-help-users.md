# Living Books

Living Books is a reader-facing experience for long-form content. Use it when you are opening, browsing, or reading a book-like artifact inside the authenticated app.

## What you can do here

- Open a living book by identifier
- Move from overview into active reading mode
- Stay inside a focused reading surface instead of an authoring interface

## Current limitations

- Living Books is a reading experience, not a writing or editorial one.
- Some discovery, organization, or related-content affordances may still live outside the reading page itself.

## Related features

- **Writing** — Long-form reading surfaces often connect back to authored content.
- **Help** — Use Help Hub when you need broader documentation rather than focused reading.
