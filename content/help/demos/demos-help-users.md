# Demos

Demo pages are showcase and reference surfaces for UI patterns, presentation experiments, and internal examples. They are not usually core production workflows, but they can be useful for reviewing patterns or testing how a concept is presented.

## What to expect

- Focused examples rather than full product workflows
- Isolated views that highlight a component, layout, or presentation style
- Faster iteration and less user-facing framing than production pages

## Current limitations

- Demo pages may change quickly and are not always documented to the same depth as stable product surfaces.
- Actions on a demo page may not map directly to a production workflow.

## Related features

- **Help** — Use Help Hub when you need to step out of a demo and review the broader product surface.
- **Dashboard** — Demo pages are usually entered intentionally, not as a day-to-day home base.
