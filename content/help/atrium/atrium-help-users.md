# Atrium

Atrium is where you hold AI Dialogues — ongoing conversations with Claude or Codex that live alongside the rest of your Mixtape work.

Atrium requires a connected AI account (Settings → AI accounts). Without one signed in and enabled, Dialogues won't start.

---

## What you can do here

- Start or continue a Dialogue with Claude or Codex
- Choose "Who replies" for each message — each provider sees what the others said
- Search your Dialogues by title or by what was said
- Distill a Dialogue to save notes before summarizing it
- Compact a long Dialogue into a checkpoint so later replies stay quick and focused
- Use quick commands, such as `/find <query>`, from the compose bar

---

## Key concepts

**Dialogue** — A single ongoing conversation, made up of messages from you and one or more AI providers.

**Who replies** — A per-message choice of which AI provider (Claude or Codex) answers next. Each provider sees the full Dialogue so far, including what the others said.

**Distill** — Pull out and save notes from a Dialogue, separate from summarizing it.

**Compact** — Write a summary of a long Dialogue so far as a checkpoint. Later replies read the summary instead of the whole Dialogue, so they stay quick and focused. You can edit the summary, and distill first if you want to save notes before compacting.

**AI account** — The connected Claude or Codex account Atrium uses to generate replies. If it needs signing in again, or Atrium isn't enabled for it, Dialogues won't run until that's fixed in Settings → AI accounts.

---

## How to start a Dialogue

1. Open Atrium.
2. Choose who replies — Claude or Codex.
3. Type a message in the compose bar and send it (Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line).

---

## How to find an earlier Dialogue

1. Open the Search Dialogues panel from Atrium.
2. Enter what you remember about the Dialogue's title or content.
3. Choose a result to reopen that Dialogue.

---

## How to keep a long Dialogue manageable

1. Open the Distill panel if you want to save notes from the Dialogue first.
2. Open Compact to write (or accept) a checkpoint summary of the Dialogue so far.
3. Continue the Dialogue — later replies read from the checkpoint instead of the full history.

---

## Current limitations

- **Requires a connected AI account:** Atrium can't send or receive replies without one signed in and enabled for Atrium.
- **Per-message provider choice:** switching between Claude and Codex is manual, message by message.

---

## Related features

- **Radar** — Personal initiative tracking (north star, last-session notes, archive) lives in Radar, not Atrium.
- **Groups** — A group's Atrium (`/groups/[slug]/atrium`) works the same way, scoped to that group.
