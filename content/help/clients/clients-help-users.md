# Clients

Clients is a focused management surface for client-related records and workflows. It is intended for people handling structured client data rather than general member browsing.

## What you can do here

- Review client records presented by the page
- Enter or update client-related workflow state
- Use a narrower operational surface without leaving the authenticated app

## Current limitations

- This area is still lightly documented and may expose workflow-specific language.
- Some supporting context may still live in adjacent operational docs or team norms rather than on the page itself.

## Related features

- **Admin** — Client pages may be used alongside operator-facing workflows.
- **Dashboard** — You may enter Clients from a broader coordination surface.
