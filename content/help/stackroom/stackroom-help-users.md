# Stackroom

Stackroom is a group's document library. It stores the documents, reference material, and resources that the group wants to keep accessible over time. What makes it different from a simple file folder is its search: Stackroom understands meaning, not just keywords, so you can find a document by describing what you're looking for rather than remembering exactly what it's called.

---

## What you can do here

- Upload documents to the group's library
- Browse the group's document collection
- Search for documents by meaning — describe what you're looking for in plain language
- Access documents shared by other group members

---

## Key concepts

**Library** — Each group has its own Stackroom library. Documents in one group's Stackroom are separate from another group's.

**Document** — Any file or text you upload. Stackroom processes documents to understand their content and make them searchable.

**Semantic search** — Stackroom can find documents based on meaning. If you search "how we handle new member onboarding," it will find relevant documents even if they don't contain those exact words.

**Namespace** — The technical term for a group's library space. Each group's documents are fully isolated from other groups.

---

## How to upload a document

1. Open Stackroom from the group's navigation (look for **Library** or **Stackroom**)
2. Click or tap **Upload**
3. Select the file from your device
4. Stackroom processes the document in the background — it will be searchable shortly after upload

---

## How to search

1. Open Stackroom
2. Type your query in the search bar — describe what you're looking for in plain language
3. Results appear ranked by relevance to your description

You can use natural language: "our founding documents," "meeting notes from last quarter," "the onboarding checklist" — Stackroom finds what matches best, not just exact text matches.

---

## Current limitations

- **Supported file types:** Depends on what your group's Stackroom configuration supports — contact your group admin if a file type you need isn't accepted
- **Processing time:** Newly uploaded documents are not immediately searchable — processing typically takes a few seconds to a minute
- **Cross-group search:** You can only search within your current group's library. There is no cross-group document search in v0.

---

## Related features

- **Collections** — A curated subset of Stackroom documents organized by your group's admins for easier browsing. Think of Collections as featured reading lists drawn from the full library.
- **Workbench** — Where documents go for review and routing before being added to the library
- **Writing** — Published writing pieces live in Writing, not Stackroom. Stackroom is for reference documents and resources.
