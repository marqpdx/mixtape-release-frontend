# Radar

Radar is a personal initiative workspace. It helps you see what is active, remember where you left off, and resume work without rebuilding context from scratch.

Atrium is the entry point for this surface. It shows your current orientation, recent initiative activity, and quick ways to start or resume work.

---

## What you can do here

- Review active initiatives from Atrium or your Radar list
- Start a new initiative with a name and direction
- Open an initiative workspace and update its north star or last-session note
- Attach supporting artifacts, such as document links or imported conversations
- Pause, activate, archive, or restore initiatives as their status changes

---

## Key concepts

**Atrium** — The orientation hub that helps you re-enter your current work.

**Initiative** — A focused piece of work you want to keep visible over time.

**North Star** — A short statement of why the initiative matters and where it is headed.

**Last Session** — A note about where you left off, so the next return starts with context.

**Archive** — A place for initiatives that are complete or no longer active. Archived initiatives can be restored.

---

## How to start an initiative

1. Open Atrium or go to your Radar list.
2. Choose the option to start a new initiative.
3. Enter a name and, if helpful, a one-sentence direction.
4. Open the initiative workspace to add notes, artifacts, and next context.

---

## How to resume work

1. Open Atrium.
2. Review the resume and orientation cards.
3. Choose the initiative you want to continue.
4. Read the north star and last-session note before adding new work.

---

## Current limitations

- **Radar is still a focused workspace:** It tracks orientation, notes, status, and artifacts, but it is not a full project management system.
- **Some context is manual:** North star and last-session notes are most useful when you keep them current.

---

## Related features

- **Initiatives** — Group initiatives support structured collaborative work.
- **Gather** — Live sessions can support real-time discussion around active work.
- **Workbench** — Operational tasks and reminders live outside Radar when they need workflow tracking.
