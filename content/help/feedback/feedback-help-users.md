# Feedback

Feedback surfaces collect structured input about the product and its workflows. Use them when you are reviewing a checklist, capturing issues, or giving targeted feedback rather than doing the primary work itself.

## What you can do here

- Complete structured checklists
- Record review input
- Capture issues, questions, or follow-up items in a purpose-built form

## Current limitations

- Feedback pages are often narrow by design and may not explain the wider workflow around them.
- Some follow-up actions still happen in a different operational surface after the checklist is completed.

## Related features

- **Dashboard** — You may discover feedback tasks from your main re-entry surface.
- **Help** — Help Hub is the better place for broad orientation if the checklist itself is not enough.
